01 · Brief

The brief

Stornoway House is a [early 19th-century] stucco building overlooking Green Park. Weathering had left the façade paintwork failing and several sashes with decayed timber at the sills and bottom rails.

We were appointed by [main contractor] to redecorate the exterior, repair the sash windows in place rather than replace them, renew the terrace decking and bring the railings back to good order. The building stayed [occupied / in use] throughout.

03 · Method

How we repair a sash window

Most decay in a sash window starts where water sits. Rather than replace the window, we cut out only the rotten timber and splice in new wood using the Repair Care system, so the original sashes, glass and mouldings stay in the building.

Why: surveyors and building owners often assume a rotten sash means a new window. One drawing shows why repair is possible and what “Repair Care trained” actually means. The same diagram would anchor a dedicated sash windows page.
